Discovery signals
Feed and Search never dead-end: after friends comes a trending stream ranked by cooks and tagged with where it is moving (NYT trending, TikTok, Instagram, friends of friends, openRCP). Signals are simulated here; in the product they come from source pages, social listening and openRCP's own log velocity.
How sharing works
The share sheet is the barcode scanner. openRCP ships a share extension (iOS) and share target (Android), so it appears in the share sheet of NYT Cooking, Safari, Chrome and Instagram. It receives only a URL, reads the page's public title, author and photo, matches the catalog and opens the Made it sheet. Paste a link is the same path by hand.
